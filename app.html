The rules that keep you off the news

The 4:1 rule sets the lean: base out one meter for every four up, 76 degrees. The top two steps of a step ladder and top three rungs of an extension are not steps - they are the part between you and the ground when the ladder walks. Stepping onto a roof, the ladder must run three rungs (1 m) past the edge so you have a handhold. And your ladder reaches 1.7 m less than the work: your arms do the last bit. Falls from ladders kill more DIYers than every power tool combined; the math is free.
